## 🚀 Projects

<table>

    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3><a href="https://github.com/SmallCoral/Coral-C5">Coral-C5</a></h3>
      <p><strong>Modular STM32C5A3 development board</strong></p>
      <p>A custom board family built around STM32C5A3VGT6, with a core board, expansion board, and a dual-motor robotics expansion.</p>
      <p>
        <img alt="STM32C5" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/MCU-STM32C5-03234B?style=flat-square" />

      </p>
      <p><sub>Core PCB has passed the latest DRC review; expansion-board work is ongoing.</sub></p>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3><a href="https://github.com/SmallCoral/Coral-Wave">Coral-Wave</a></h3>
      <p><strong>USB audio interface</strong></p>
      <p>A USB sound card based on ATSAMD21E18A and WM8978, with headphone, microphone, and speaker interfaces, plus a Windows control utility.</p>
      <p>
        <img alt="ATSAMD21" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/MCU-ATSAMD21-007ACC?style=flat-square" />
        <img alt="USB Audio" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Audio-USB%20Audio-0F766E?style=flat-square" />
        <img alt="WM8978" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Codec-WM8978-7C3AED?style=flat-square" />
      </p>
      <p><sub>Board-level playback and microphone capture have been tested successfully at 48 kHz.</sub></p>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>FPGA Robot</h3>
      <p><strong>Video pipeline and autonomous obstacle avoidance</strong></p>
      <p>Developing an FPGA car with camera data buffered through DDR3 to a 480×272 TFT, Ethernet-based debugging, and LD06 LiDAR obstacle avoidance.</p>
      <p>
        <img alt="FPGA" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-FPGA-7C3AED?style=flat-square" />

      </p>
    </td>
